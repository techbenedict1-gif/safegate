What is collected, and why

  • Your name, matric number and phone number, and an emergency contact. So the school knows who you are and who to call in an emergency.
  • When you leave and enter the gate, and where you said you were going. This replaces the paper register and helps find someone who goes missing.
  • Your location, only if you turn it on, only while you are outside after the evening tracking time, until you scan back in. Pressing the red emergency button shares your live location at once.
  • Reports, photos, voice notes and videos you send to security, only when you choose to send them.
  • Health card (optional): shown to responders only during your own emergency.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We show no ads and nothing is for sale to students
  • Your location is never used for anything except your safety.
  • Officers cannot open your location without choosing a reason. Every look is recorded and shown to you in the app.

How long it is kept

    Your rights, and how to use them

    • See your data: in the app, Me & privacy, Download my data.
    • Take back your location data: Erase my location history.
    • Delete your account: Me & privacy, Delete my whole account. Safety records such as past emergencies stay with the school for its legal duty, but nothing in them points to you.
    • Change your mind: turn location sharing off at any time. It is as easy to turn off as on.
    • Correct your details: Me & privacy, Edit my details.
    • Under 18: your location stays off until the school records your parent or guardian's agreement.
    • Complain: contact us below, or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).
