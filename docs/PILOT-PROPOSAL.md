## 1. The problem

Students who live off campus, or leave it, sign a paper register with vague destinations such as "market". If a student does not return, the register cannot say where to start looking, and it cannot be searched. Parents cannot tell whether a late student is safe. A lost phone or a full register page means no record at all.

## 2. What I am proposing

A **one-term pilot** of SafeGate, a campus safety and movement system I built:

- Students scan a QR code at the gate (or the guard scans their ID card) instead of writing in a register. It works **without internet** and syncs later. A paper log stays as the fallback.
- Students say where they are going and when they will be back. Overdue students appear on a **live security board**, and security is alerted automatically.
- A student can press an **emergency button**, ask for an **escort**, or start a **safe-walk**. Security can see the student's location **only** with the student's consent and only when they are outside after [5:30 PM].
- Parents who choose to can get **text updates** (left, back, late, emergency). Parents pay a few naira per text themselves. **The school pays nothing for this.**
- Security gets records, case tracking, visitor registration, exit-pass approvals, drills, and reports.

## 3. What I need from the school

1. Permission to run the pilot at [one gate / one hostel / one department] from [date] to [date].
2. A named contact in Security: [name].
3. An instruction to guards and students at the pilot location to use SafeGate (with the paper log still available).
4. A short written agreement on data protection (see section 5).

## 4. Cost to the school

**₦0** for the pilot. Students use SafeGate free. Texts to parents are paid by parents. Emergency texts to staff and parents are covered from a small reserve that I fund during the pilot.

After the pilot, if the school wants to continue, we discuss a simple arrangement (hosting and support, with texts paid by usage). The school decides based on the results below.

## 5. Privacy and safety

- Written permission from the school before any student data is collected.
- Students give consent for location in the app and can turn it off. Location is never sent to parents.
- Data is kept on a server controlled by [me / the school], protected by staff PINs, with an audit log of who looked at what. Students can download or erase their data.
- I will follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act. The school remains the controller of its students' data.

| Measure | Paper register today | Target in the pilot |

| Time to sign a student out | [measure it] | under 5 seconds |

SafeGate records these automatically (Analytics tab and drill results), so the report to the school at the end of the term is real data.
