# SafeGate strategy: free for students, loved because it solves real problems

## The promise
SafeGate is **free for every student, always**. No ads, no Plus, nothing to buy, no selling or sharing of data. This is a setting (ICT > System > Settings > "Free for every student") and it is **on by default**. Safety features are never charged for.

## Why people will use it and keep using it
A feature list does not make people love an app. Removing a daily pain does. Each part of SafeGate is there for one problem:

| Problem students and the school have | What SafeGate does about it |
|---|---|
| Queuing and writing in a paper register at the gate | Scan once, two seconds. Forgot your phone or ID, or in a hurry or an emergency? Nobody is stopped. |
| "Where is my child / my friend?" and nobody knows for days | A clear last-known record, and a trail only with the student's agreement |
| Walking back late and alone | Safe-walk: security is watching until you arrive |
| Danger | Red button: security sees your live location at once; quiet duress code; parents and security told |
| Reports that vanish into a void | You see: sent, received, seen, being handled, help on the way, resolved |
| Fear of being watched | You see every time an officer looked at your location, and why. You can turn it off, download it or delete it |
| Bad network, cheap phones | Works offline, syncs later, light on data |
| Phone lost or changed | Your account is safe on the server and moves to a new phone with a code, or with an officer's approval |

People keep using what saves them time and makes them feel safe without feeling watched. So every screen is judged by two questions: does it remove a pain, and does it make the student feel respected?

## How it stays free (honest costs)
- **Running it:** your phone costs nothing extra. A small server (VPS, about 5 US dollars a month) is safer for a whole school.
- **The only real cost is SMS.** Push notifications are free. Use SMS only for emergencies and parents who choose it. Turn parent texts on only if the school pays.
- **Who can pay, without charging students or selling data:** the school (ICT or student welfare budget, because it replaces a paper process and reduces risk), the Students' Union or alumni as a voluntary contribution, campus welfare sponsors who pay the school directly (no ads inside the app), and safety grants. Ask the school to treat it like any other campus security service.
- Optional paid extras exist in the operator console (`docs/MONETIZATION.md`), but they are **off** while the free setting is on. Turn them on only if the school agrees.

## How to get a school to adopt it
1. **Pilot one gate and one hostel for a month.** Small, low risk, easy to say yes to.
2. **Measure three numbers:** time to get through the gate, time from a report to "help on the way", and how many students signed up.
3. **Get the Students' Union and security chief on your side first.** They decide if it lives or dies.
4. **Start Relaxed** (Gate policy), then move to Balanced once students are used to it. Use the school's own ID rule.
5. **Show the privacy notice (`/privacy`) in the first meeting.** It is the thing that makes a school and parents comfortable.
6. **Print the poster**, put it behind glass at the gate, and keep paper as the backup so nothing ever depends on the app.

## What can still go wrong
- **One phone is the weak point.** If the phone running SafeGate dies, nothing runs, and any data since your last off-phone copy can be lost. Move to a VPS before the school depends on it.
- **A printed code can be photographed.** SafeGate makes cheating hard and visible, not impossible.
- **Adoption is the real risk.** An app students avoid is worse than paper. Keep it fast, kind and optional where policy allows.
