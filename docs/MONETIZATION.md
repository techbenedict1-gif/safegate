# SafeGate money plan

Everything below can be changed in the Operator console (**Plans & ads** tab). Prices are starting points to test, not promises. Check your own SMS cost and what students in your school can really pay before you announce anything.

## The promise (never changes)
- **Safety is free.** Emergency alerts, gate scans, reports, help requests and escorts never cost anything and never show an ad.
- **No data selling.** Location and movement are never used to choose or sell an ad. Sponsors see only totals (views, taps).
- **Ads are small, labelled "Sponsored", and can be hidden for the day.** They appear only on the Home screen, and never during an emergency, an exit, a roll-call or a privacy notice.

This is also the pitch to the school: the app is safe to put on every student's phone because the money never touches safety.

## Who pays what
| Who | What they get | Price | Why this price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **School licence** | Every student gets Plus free (no ads) | ₦1,000 per student per session | Biggest, steadiest income. First term free as a pilot, then paid once the school has seen results |
| **Parents: Family** | Up to 60 texts a month (left, back, late, emergency) | ₦1,500 a month, ₦4,000 a term | Parents have the money and the worry. 60 texts at roughly ₦5 each is about ₦300 cost, so about 80% margin. Pay-as-you-go texting stays for anyone who does not want a plan |
| **Students: Plus** | No ads, 3x evidence storage, badge, supports the free app | ₦300 a month, ₦800 a term | Deliberately small. Students are price-sensitive. The term price is about a third cheaper than paying monthly |
| **Local sponsors** | A card on the student Home screen | Starter ₦10,000 a month, Campus Partner ₦30,000 a month, Launch week ₦5,000 | Food, printing, data, pharmacies and phone repair near campus want students. A flat monthly fee is simple for them |

Own ad network (AdSense etc.) is **not** used: it tracks students, can show unsafe ads, and pays almost nothing at campus scale. Selling to local sellers pays more and you control what appears.

## Ads you refuse
Betting, loans, alcohol, adult content, anything that pressures students for money, and anything the school objects to. Check every ad before switching it on.

## Rollout
1. **Weeks 1 to 4, pilot:** everything free. Ads off, billing off. Collect proof: reports handled, how fast help came, forgotten-ID exits that went fine.
2. **Month 2, parents:** switch on pay-as-you-go texting and Family. Take payment by bank transfer and record it in the console (Paystack can come later).
3. **Month 3, sponsors:** sign 2 or 3 local sellers using the rate card, then switch ads on. Show each sponsor their views and taps.
4. **Next term, school:** take the pilot numbers to management and offer the licence. A licensed campus has no ads and every student has Plus.

## Rules for yourself
- Never put safety behind a payment, even in a "premium" screen.
- Do not announce a price you have not tested with ten real students and five parents.
- Tell the school in writing that ads and subscriptions exist, and who gets the money, before you switch them on.
- Keep a record of every payment (the console does this) and follow Nigerian tax and data-protection rules (NDPA): a short privacy page and a registered business make schools much more willing to sign.
